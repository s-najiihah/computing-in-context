# Najiihah's Portfolio

I'm a public policy professional and MPA student at LSE and Columbia University, 
specialising in data science and technology policy. This portfolio showcases my 
data analysis projects from Advanced Computing in Context at Columbia University, 
taught by Professor Aidan Feldman.

## Projects

**[NYC Building Insights](nyc_building_insights)**
An interactive Streamlit dashboard exploring NYC building permits, evictions, and 
complaints across all five boroughs.

**[KTMB Ridership Trends, 2020 to 2025](project_1)**
Analysis of daily ridership data across Malaysia's five main KTMB rail services, 
including post-pandemic recovery trends.

**[GDP and CO2 Emissions Per Capita, 2020 to 2025](project_2)**
Exploring the relationship between national wealth and carbon emissions across 
countries, using data from Our World in Data.

**[McDonald's Locations and Global Obesity Rates](project_3)**
Exploring the relationship between McDonald's locations and adult obesity rates 
across 90+ countries, visualised as an interactive choropleth map.
